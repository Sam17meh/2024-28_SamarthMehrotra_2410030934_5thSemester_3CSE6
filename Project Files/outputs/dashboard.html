Read the agreement rate as a feature, not a shortfall. The recorded diagnoses were produced without access to the expected_fault column, and wrong answers were left in rather than regenerated until they disappeared. A dashboard reporting 100% agreement would be evidence of a leaked answer key. The 12 corrections are the deliverable; they are documented case by case in docs/responsible_ai_log.md.
